# Local benchmark for an RFP requirements-and-change review service

**Status: unvalidated experiment package. No initiative has been launched and no test results are claimed.**

## Audience and problem

Small U.S. web-development agencies preparing public-sector website proposals.

Required response items and changes may be scattered across RFPs, Q&amp;A documents, and amendments. Missing them could jeopardize a bid, but prevalence and demand for outside review remain unknown.

## Hypothesis

A source-linked review workflow can produce a requirements-and-change checklist for each of three closed website-RFP packages with 100% critical-requirement recall, at least 95% overall recall, no invented mandatory obligations, correct citations for every accepted row, and no more than 30 minutes of correction per package.

## Smallest test

Prepare—not execute or launch—a $0, seven-day local benchmark protocol capped at eight founder hours. Specify selection of three complete, closed public website-RFP packages containing an original solicitation and at least one amendment or Q&amp;A; verify primary documents before using them. Freeze package selection and a rubric defining critical items as explicit eligibility, submission, deadline, disqualification, or amendment-acknowledgment requirements. Build and freeze manual reference checklists separately from candidate generation; withhold reference labels from the candidate workflow. Generate one checklist per package containing obligations, source/page citations, changes, and unresolved ambiguities. Score recall before correction and log correction time separately. Deliver the protocol, selection manifest template, checklist template, scoring sheet, and decision rules. References are fallible founder-created fixtures; this test cannot establish customer benefit, superiority to existing tools, or willingness to pay.

## Criterion recorded before execution

RFP packages satisfying every preregistered hypothesis gate: 3 packages out of 3 within 7 days.

## Stop condition

Stop at seven days or eight founder hours without extending thresholds. If complete primary documents or defensible reference labels cannot be obtained, record the benchmark as inconclusive. Any package missing a gate means the hypothesis fails; do not replace failed packages or launch a service on this evidence.

## Intended prototype behavior

- Use a local, non-deployed review workflow on three complete public website-RFP packages, each containing the original solicitation and at least one amendment or Q&amp;A. Verify primary documents before admitting a package; discovery summaries are not benchmark inputs.
- Produce one requirements-and-change checklist per package. Row template: package ID; requirement ID; obligation; mandatory or advisory status; critical category, if applicable; source file/version; page and section or question; supporting excerpt; change and supersession relationship; unresolved ambiguity.
- Identify explicit eligibility, submission, deadline, disqualification, and amendment-acknowledgment requirements as critical. Reconcile changes using documented authority and chronology; flag unresolved conflicts instead of inventing mandatory obligations.
- Generate candidates without access to the frozen manual reference checklists. Preserve the original output before scoring. Corrected outputs are separate artifacts and cannot replace pre-correction scores.

## Test procedure

- 1. Preregister the protocol before any run: seven-day window, $0 budget, eight-hour cap, selection rules, rubric, candidate workflow version, matching rules, gates, and decision rules. Keep this as the sole active experiment. No execution is authorized by this record.
- 2. Select and verify sources \(60 minutes budget\). Use a recorded discovery order and admit the first three eligible complete, closed public-sector website-RFP packages. Verify official closure evidence and the issuing authority’s document listing. Manifest template: package ID; issuer; project; official URLs; closure evidence; document inventory; amendment sequence; retrieval date; file hash/version; completeness rationale; inclusion or exclusion reason. Freeze selection before candidate generation.
- 3. Build and freeze manual references separately \(150 minutes\). Read all package documents and record atomic, deduplicated response obligations, critical labels, citations, and change relationships. Treat conditional duties as conditional and superseded duties as historical. Record ambiguous interpretations explicitly. Withhold these fixtures from candidate generation.
- 4. Generate one candidate checklist per package \(60 minutes\). Freeze the workflow configuration across all three packages. Save inputs, configuration, raw outputs, failures, and elapsed time; do not tune or rerun against revealed labels.
- 5. Score untouched outputs against the frozen references \(90 minutes\). Match rows by substantive obligation and applicable conditions, not wording. Count duplicates once. An ambiguity flag alone does not count as a recovered obligation. Inspect every candidate row for invented mandatory duties and citation correctness. Log reference disputes without silently changing fixtures.
- 6. Correct each output while timing active work separately \(90 minutes total; no more than 30 minutes per package to pass\). Include missing-row additions, unsupported-duty removal, citation repair, and change reconciliation. Preserve unfinished corrections when a package exceeds its gate; correction never rescues a failed raw-output gate.
- 7. Reserve the final 30 minutes within the eight-hour cap to archive sources, fixtures, outputs, discrepancy logs, time records, and the decision. Stop sooner if seven days or eight founder hours is reached. Report measured results only if execution later occurs.

## Acceptance checks

- Fixed primary metric: RFP packages satisfying every preregistered hypothesis gate. Fixed target: 3 packages out of 3. Fixed window: 7 days.
- Each package must achieve 100% critical-requirement recall and at least 95% overall recall before correction. Recall equals distinct reference obligations correctly recovered divided by applicable reference obligations; compute critical recall on the critical subset. Record denominators and mark empty subsets as not applicable rather than inventing a percentage.
- Each package must contain zero invented mandatory obligations and correct citations for every accepted row. Accepted rows are candidate assertions presented as usable checklist entries, not a post-scoring selection that excludes errors. Citations must resolve to the frozen source and support the obligation, conditions, and claimed change.
- Each package must require no more than 30 minutes of correction. Record all correction work separately from candidate generation and initial scoring.
- Stop at seven days or eight founder hours without extending thresholds. If complete primary documents or defensible reference labels cannot be obtained, record the benchmark as inconclusive. Any package missing a gate means the hypothesis fails; do not replace failed packages or launch a service on this evidence.
- A pass supports only internal feasibility on these three packages. Any revision or customer-facing test requires a new recorded protocol.

No agency has been recruited or represented as a customer. Recruitment for a later permitted usability study would require a separate protocol.

## Limitations

- Supplied observations are overlapping model-written research summaries, not independently verified customer accounts or seven independent confirmations.
- Founder-created references are fallible fixtures. Withholding labels from candidate inputs reduces leakage but does not provide independent human blinding.
- Three closed, publicly accessible website-RFP packages cannot establish general reliability, particularly for incomplete, scanned, unusual, or actively changing procurements.
- The benchmark does not establish willingness to pay, adoption, avoided bid losses, time savings, sustainable delivery economics, or superiority to manual review or existing products.
- A checklist is review assistance, not a legal interpretation or guarantee of procurement compliance. An incomplete benchmark provides no basis for launch.
