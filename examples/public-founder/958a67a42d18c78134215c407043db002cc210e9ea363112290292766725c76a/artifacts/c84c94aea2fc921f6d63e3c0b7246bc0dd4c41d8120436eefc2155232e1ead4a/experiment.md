# First experiment: example-founder

**Status: unvalidated experiment package. No initiative has been launched and no test results are claimed.**

## Audience and problem

Not selected. Identify who would benefit before committing to an experiment.

The need addressed by this mission remains untested: Develop a venture consistent with the configured mission. Identify assumptions, gather evidence, propose bounded experiments, and retain what is learned.

## Hypothesis

A small facilitated pilot may establish whether the intended audience can achieve a useful outcome related to the mission.

## Smallest test

Facilitate one representative audience activity manually before committing to a full venture.

## Criterion recorded before execution

Participants completing the agreed activity without facilitator intervention: 3 participants out of 5 recruited within 7 days.

## Stop condition

Stop if the need is not recurring or fewer than three of five participants complete the activity.

## Intended prototype behavior

- Specify one audience activity related to the founder mission.
- Provide the minimum manual pilot needed to attempt that activity.

## Test procedure

- Obtain agreement from five relevant participants for a future test.
- Define one representative activity and a clear completion condition.
- Observe each participant attempt the activity.
- Record outcomes against the original criterion.

## Acceptance checks

- The activity has a clear completion condition.
- The observer can tell whether facilitator intervention was needed.

## Recruitment plan
